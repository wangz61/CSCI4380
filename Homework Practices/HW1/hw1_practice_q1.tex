\section*{Practice Problem for Homework 1 Question 1}

\textbf{(Practice Problem for Question 1)}  
Find the first name and last name of all passengers who have ever flown on a flight that departed from an airport located in the city of \emph{Los Angeles} and arrived at an airport located in the city of \emph{New York}.  
Cities may have multiple airports. Return each passenger only once, even if they took multiple such flights.

\subsection*{Answer:}


\paragraph{Step 1: Identify Los Angeles airports.}
\[
LA \;=\; \sigma_{\text{City} = 'Los\ Angeles'}(\text{Airports})
\]

\paragraph{Step 2: Identify New York airports.}
\[
NY \;=\; \sigma_{\text{City} = 'New\ York'}(\text{Airports})
\]

\paragraph{Step 3: Find all flights departing from Los Angeles airports.}
\[
FromLA \;=\; \text{Flights} \;\bowtie_{\text{DepartureAirportCode} = \text{Code}}\; LA
\]

\paragraph{Step 4: From those, keep only flights arriving at New York airports.}
\[
LAtoNY \;=\; FromLA \;\bowtie_{\text{ArrivalAirportCode} = \text{Code}}\; NY
\]

\paragraph{Step 5: Retrieve the passengers who flew on those flights.}

We first connect flights to tickets, and then tickets to passengers:

\[
Pax \;=\; \text{Passengers} \;\bowtie\; \text{Tickets} \;\bowtie\; \text{TicketFlights} \;\bowtie_{\text{TicketFlights.FId} = LAtoNY.FId}\; LAtoNY
\]

\paragraph{Step 6: Project the required attributes and remove duplicates.}

\[
\pi_{\text{FirstName}, \text{LastName}}(Pax)
\]

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\pi_{\text{FirstName}, \text{LastName}} \Big(
&\text{Passengers}
\;\bowtie\;
\text{Tickets}
\;\bowtie\;
\text{TicketFlights}
\;\bowtie_{\text{TicketFlights.FId} = LAtoNY.FId} \\
&\Big(
(\text{Flights}
\;\bowtie_{\text{DepartureAirportCode} = \text{Code}}\;
\sigma_{\text{City} = 'Los\ Angeles'}(\text{Airports})) \\
&\qquad\bowtie_{\text{ArrivalAirportCode} = \text{Code}}\;
\sigma_{\text{City} = 'New\ York'}(\text{Airports})
\Big)
\Big)
\end{aligned}
}
\]

This expression returns the first and last names of all distinct passengers who have flown on at least one flight departing from Los Angeles and arriving in New York.
